# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This repository represents the professional profile and public-facing work of Er. Tapan Jatakia. Everyone participating in its issues, pull requests, reviews, and other repository interactions is expected to act professionally, respectfully, and constructively.

We aim to provide a welcoming environment regardless of background, experience, identity, or point of view.

## Expected behaviour

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Communicating respectfully and in good faith.
- Giving constructive, specific feedback.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Accepting reasonable editorial decisions and maintainer feedback.
- Protecting privacy and respecting intellectual-property rights.
- Keeping discussions relevant to the repository and its stated purpose.

## Unacceptable behaviour

The following conduct is not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory, hateful, or deliberately degrading language.
- Doxxing, publishing private information, or sharing confidential communications without permission.
- Impersonating the profile owner or misrepresenting another person's identity, credentials, or endorsement.
- Spam, abusive promotion, or deliberately disruptive behaviour.
- Submitting malicious code, unsafe links, deceptive content, or material intended to compromise users.
- Making unauthorised changes or claims about the profile owner's professional history, qualifications, affiliations, or views.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to repository issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing or interacting with this repository.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct that violates this policy by contacting the repository owner through the contact options on the owner's GitHub profile. Avoid posting sensitive or identifying information in a public issue.

The repository owner will review reports as promptly as reasonably possible and will handle them with discretion. Depending on the circumstances, responses may include a warning, removal or editing of content, closure of an issue or pull request, restriction of repository participation, or a block from further interaction.

The repository owner is responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct. Enforcement decisions will be proportionate to the nature and severity of the conduct.

## Acknowledgement

Participation in this repository constitutes agreement to follow this Code of Conduct.
